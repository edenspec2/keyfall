Privacy policy

Mochi MIDI, an app for learning piano on your MIDI controller, in your browser. Last updated 28 October 2026.

The short version

There is no account, no advertising and no tracking of you as a person. Your lessons, songs and settings stay on your device. During the beta, the app may send anonymous counts of how lessons are used, and you can switch that off.

What stays on your device

  • Your lesson progress, practice history, recorded songs, sound settings and preferences are saved in your browser's storage on this device. They are never uploaded. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them.
  • Your MIDI keyboard's notes and controls, and all sound, are handled inside your browser and never leave your device.
  • If you install the app, a copy of its files and piano sounds is kept on your device so it opens without a connection.

Anonymous usage counts

To see whether the app works for people, it may count, using GoatCounter:

  • a visit to the app;
  • a lesson started, and a lesson passed (with the lesson's short name, such as "first-tune");
  • the sound failing to start.

Nothing about you is sent: no name, no account, nothing you play or type. GoatCounter does not store IP addresses and stores nothing in your browser (no cookies). It keeps only totals, such as the browser, the screen width and the country, which it works out from the connection without keeping the IP address.

You can switch the counts off in Settings → About.

Feedback

If you use the "Send feedback" form, what you type is sent to the form service and read by the developer to fix and improve the app. Don't include anything you would rather keep private.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: open an issue at github.com/edenspec2/mochimidi.
